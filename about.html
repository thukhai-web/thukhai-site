About ThuKhai

ThuKhai is a review site for people buying their first cordless power tools. We focus on DIY beginners and homeowners — the people who need one drill that does the job, not a workshop full of pro gear.

Most tool content is written for contractors or copied from spec sheets. We do something different: we explain what the specs mean for real weekend projects, compare the battery platforms that lock you in for years, and tell you when a cheaper tool is genuinely enough.

How we make money

ThuKhai is not currently part of any affiliate program, so there are no affiliate links on this site and we do not earn commissions from purchases. If that changes, our rule will stay the same: commissions will never affect our rankings or recommendations. See our full affiliate disclosure.

Corrections

Tool models and prices change often. If you spot an error, tell us and we will fix it.
